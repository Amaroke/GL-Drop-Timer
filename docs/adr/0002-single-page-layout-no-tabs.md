# Single page with the Planner always visible, no tabs

Spec 3 originally planned two tabs, Drops and Colonies, with an account menu at the top right. Designing the space for the Planner (the future Colonies and Buildings progression feature) surfaced a simpler shape: keep everything on one screen, the three Drop timers in a header row and the Planner reserved below, with the account and notification controls tucked into small icon-triggered popups in the top corners. This keeps both tools visible together, the way a player actually uses them, and avoids introducing a tab or routing concept for what is, so far, only two views.

## Considered Options

- Two tabs, Drops and Colonies: keeps each view focused, but hides the Planner until the player switches tabs and needs a navigation concept the app has avoided until now.
